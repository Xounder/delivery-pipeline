# Phase 11 — Provider Reliability Layer

## Goal

Create a resilient provider ecosystem.

---

## Features

### Timeout Management

Prevent slow providers from blocking searches.

---

### Retry Management

Retry temporary failures.

---

### Circuit Breaker

Temporarily disable unstable providers.

---

### Health Monitoring

Track:

- Availability
- Latency
- Failure rate

---

### Provider Scoring

Track provider quality and stability.

---

## Success Criteria

Provider failures do not affect platform usability.

---
